# In the Beginning, in the Midst, at the End / ஆதியிலே, பாதியிலே, முடிவிலே
## Father, Son, Spirit — from the Word to the whirlwind

A bilingual Scripture songbook from Genesis to the New Testament. Beginning: the Word with God. Midst: Jesus revealed in the flesh, crucified, risen, and coming again. End: the trumpet, the rolled heaven, and the Lord who does not delay. Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Miracles from Egypt to Elisha, and the healings, deliverances, and raisings of Jesus. English cinematic companion, Tamil original, and a scan of the handwritten pages. Sense kept. KJC-DNN.
